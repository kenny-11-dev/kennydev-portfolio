About me

I'm Kenny, a full-stack web developer based in Rwanda. I build websites and web applications for businesses, startups and individuals who want a stronger presence online.

I like turning real business problems into simple, usable software. I start by understanding how your business works and who your customers are, then build something clear, fast and easy to maintain.

Every site I build is designed to work well on phones, tablets and desktops, because most Rwandan customers browse on mobile.
